Koma nimaana ki Jawab kaafi nahi tha Lekhi nphi likhha ki RTI kanun ke tha hai wahi jaankari vihjahasakhti hai June material form me mojudho aur sabh sahem baat Ononi likhha ki Chunabhayok ne abhi to k Pure Deshme S.I.R. Karwane ka koi fesle nahi hi liya hai isle koi jaankari nahi vihjahasakhti Lekhi kese hoosakhta hai Chobhi June 2015 ko apne aadeesh me Chunabhayok ne Pure Deshme S.I.R.
